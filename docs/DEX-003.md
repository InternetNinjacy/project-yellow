## Asset contract

- Target 16x16 overworld actor frames, four cardinal facings, with horizontal flip for mirrored right pose as engine permits.
- Existing verified sprite format: 12 tiles / 192 bytes of indexed 2bpp data, distributed in the 16x48 source sheet matching Bulbasaur and Ivysaur.
- Keep large Venusaur silhouette legible at Game Boy resolution: squat quadruped, large distinctive flower and foliage, broad shoulders and unmistakable outline. Confirm flower/leaf readability, avoid removing canonical defining features merely to fit.
- Create and version a concept preview and pixel asset separately. Obtain explicit visual approval before production merge. Track raw source PNG, 2bpp export, checksum, preview, and emulator screenshots.
